ReKindle is an immersive group experience about reconnecting with the people around
you. Participants scan a QR code to join a shared "Vibe Room," pair up, and bring the
moment to life on a large projected display by uploading photos of friends and sending
words and phrases that surface together on screen. Instead of another thing to stare
at alone on a phone, it turns a room full of people into a single shared, playful
canvas.

Built as a vibe coding experience.
